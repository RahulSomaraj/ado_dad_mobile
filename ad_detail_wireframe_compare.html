What changes — and why

Six focused deltas. Most of the page (gallery, EMI, sticky action bar) already shipped in recent builds; the proposal sharpens scannability and trust.
1
Current
Category tag overlaid on the gallery; no seller-trust marker on the image.
Proposed
Verified-seller badge floats on the gallery so trust registers before the buyer scrolls a pixel.
2
Current
Price with EMI on a second line as muted text.
Proposed
EMI promoted to a chip beside the price — reads as a feature, not fine print, and frees a line.
3
Current
Specs as a stacked key–value list (one row per fact) — long vertical scroll before the description.
Proposed
The five facts buyers want — year · fuel · transmission · km · owner — as one-tap chips, scannable at a glance. (Code already moved to _specChips; proposal finalizes it.)
4
Current
Seller row shows name + "Member since" only; not tappable to a profile.
Proposed
Adds rating, typical reply time, ad count and opens the full seller profile — the signals that decide whether to message.
5
Current
"Similar ads" — generic recommendations, no distance or "see all".
Proposed
"Similar near you" with distance on each card + a See-all entry — leverages the location data the home feed already uses.
6
Current
Sticky bar order: Call · Make an Offer · Chat.
Proposed
Reorder to Chat · Make an Offer · Call — leads with the lowest-friction action; Call (highest intent) anchors the right. Bar already sticky in current build.
