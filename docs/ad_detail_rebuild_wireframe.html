Ad detail, rebuilt around the decision

The detail page is where a listing either earns a call or gets swiped away. Today it renders every category through one vehicle template, prints the price without separators, auto-advances the gallery while the buyer is reading it, labels the odometer “Kmpl”, and hides half of what the API already returns. These plates rework the page around the four questions a buyer actually asks — what is it, what condition, what price, who's selling — and carry the same fixes out to the card, the gallery and the states.

Ad detail · buyer · vehicle

  1. 1

    Thumbnail strip instead of dots

    Six 44 dp thumbnails under the hero, current one outlined, video shown in place with a ▶ badge, a +9 tile for the rest. Dots are tiny, closely-spaced hit areas that cause mis-taps and hide how many photos exist; a strip both signposts the count and gives a real target.

    Baymard only 24% of mobile sites use thumbnails; half of users failed to find extra images when only dots were shown. Material 48 dp minimum target.
  2. 2

    Kill the auto-advance and the baked-in gradient

    Remove the 3 s / 5 s Timer entirely — a detail gallery that moves while you're reading is hostile, and it's currently restarted from inside build. Also drop the 0x99000000 → 0xAA000000 gradient painted over every photo; replace it with a scrim only behind the control clusters, so buyers judge paint and damage on the real image.

    Today add_detail_page.dart:132, :846, :1005–1021
  3. 3

    Price is the headline, at 25 sp with separators

    Indian grouping (₹4,85,000), tabular figures, and clear separation from the title — the title drops to 14.5 sp regular. inr() already exists in rich_ad_card.dart; lift it into lib/common and use it everywhere. Rent listings keep the /mo suffix.

  4. 4

    Price context band — but only when you can back it

    “Fair price · 42 similar Swifts in Ernakulam” converts an unknowable number into a judgement, which is exactly why CarDekho ships Great / Fair / Above Market. Render it only above a comparables threshold (say ≥ 15 same model-year listings in the region) and always name the basis. A wrong “Above market” on an honest seller's ad is worse than no band at all — so it needs a backend endpoint, not a client-side guess.

  5. 5

    Price drop replaces “Posted On”

    “↓ ₹20,000 since 2 Sep” is honest urgency and signals a motivated seller. The posting date moves down to the location line as a relative time (“3 days ago”), where OLX puts it.

  6. 6

    The EMI chip goes

    price * 0.018 presented as a monthly figure is a made-up number with no rate, tenure or down payment — and you're not the lender. Either remove it, or make it a tappable “EMI from ₹8,499/mo” that opens a calculator with visible defaults (11.5% p.a. · 60 months · 20% down · indicative, not an offer). Same call applies on the card.

    RBI's digital-lending direction is toward all-in cost disclosure, not headline instalments.
  7. 7

    Five key chips, chosen by category

    Reg year · kms driven · fuel · transmission · owner number. These are the value-setting facts; everything else moves into the grouped table in Plate 2. Two tiers beats one flat Wrap where brand and mileage carry equal weight.

    Baymard only 3% of sites summarise critical specs; 50% have spec sheets users can't scan.
  8. 8

    Sticky bar: one primary, one call, one save

    Chat is the primary because it keeps the lead on-platform and maskable. Call sits beside it as a 44 dp target. “Make an offer” drops into the chat sheet (Plate 3) — three equal-weight CTAs make the highest-commitment action the loudest one. Replace the hardcoded SafeArea(minimum: 65) with the real system inset.

    Growth Rock A/B a sticky bar that opens the action gained +5.2% orders; one that merely scrolled to the CTA gained nothing.

Ad detail · scrolled

  1. 9

    Two groups, single column, alternating rows

    “Vehicle” (what it is) and “Documents & condition” (what you're buying into). Single column with alternating row tint — eye-tracking shows it improves both speed and accuracy of tracing a label across to its value. Multi-column spec blocks get misread as comparison tables.

    Baymard 23% of sites don't group specs semantically; 20% use multi-column and confuse users.
  2. 10

    “Kms driven”, never “Mileage”

    Rename the field everywhere — detail, card, post form, edit form. If you ever add real fuel economy, that's a separate field called “Mileage (kmpl)”. Fixing the label alone is a one-line change with outsized trust value.

  3. 11

    Surface RC, insurance, ownership, RTO

    hasRcBook and hasInsurance are already in the model and never rendered. Registration state and RTO code drive re-registration cost, so they belong beside the year. “Loan / hypothecation — not declared” is worth adding to the post form: an undisclosed loan is a top cause of failed C2C deals.

  4. 12

    Description truncates to 3 lines, expands in place

    Seller-written text is messy. Truncate with an inline “Read more” that expands on the same screen — never a new route. Where the free text contains structured facts (“insurance till March”, “single owner”), pull them into the table over time.

    Baymard every test user relied on the description; 78% of sites dump it as an unstructured wall.
  5. 13

    One verification signal, not three

    Today “verified” appears as a gallery badge, a purple tick on the tile, and an unused SellerStats.isVerified. Keep one badge that says what was verified — “Phone verified” — on the seller card. Vague ticks read as decoration; specific ones read as fact.

  6. 14

    The trust row needs its own states

    GET /v2/ads/sellers/:id/stats is fetched in initState and failures are swallowed, so the row silently appears late or never. Reserve its height, show a skeleton, and fall back to “Member since 2023” alone. Ship “replies in ~10 min” but instrument it — there's no published evidence that response badges lift contact rate, only widespread practice.

  7. 15

    Report and block, one tap, always visible

    Currently a right-aligned text button floating between description and seller. Attach both to the seller card where the mistrust actually forms.

  8. 16

    Safety note inline, near the CTA — not an interstitial

    The dominant fraud pattern in India is an impersonated “army officer” demanding an advance over UPI, so the copy names the behaviour rather than saying “be careful”. Keep it inline and dismissible. A full-screen safety gate before revealing contact gets dismissed blind and costs you the lead.

  9. 17

    Similar ads must actually be similar

    Same model, ±20% price, same city — and “See all” carries those filters into search. Today it opens a bare /search?from=ad-detail with nothing applied. Alternatives are what matter in classifieds; there's no such thing as a complementary purchase here.

Offer sheet

  1. 18

    Pre-filled openers, in Malayalam and English

    OLX ships saved chat templates explicitly for first-time-internet users, and it's the single cheapest lift to first-contact rate: the buyer never faces an empty text field. Four openers, one tap, editable before send.

  2. 19

    Offer presets remove the “what's a rude number?” freeze

    Asking · −5% · −10% · custom, mirroring Facebook Marketplace and eBay. Cap at 3 offers per ad per buyer (eBay's cap; Vinted uses 5/day) so sellers don't get ground down. Show the remaining count — it makes the cap feel like a rule, not a bug.

    Convergent practice across marketplaces; no published conversion data. Instrument it.
  3. 20

    Call through a masked number, and gate it

    Call is currently the one action a logged-out user can take, and it dials ad.user.phone raw, ignoring countryCode. Route calls through a virtual number so a seller's personal line is never exposed — OLX has hidden numbers by default since 2016 — and require login, like every other action.

  4. 21

    The offer sheet carries the ad, not the description

    ChatService and OfferService are handed ad.description as the title today, so chat rooms are headed by a paragraph. Pass ad.title with the price and thumbnail.

    Today add_detail_page.dart:1972, :2032
  5. 22

    Deliberate: no WhatsApp button

    WhatsApp deep-links convert well in India, but the moment the lead leaves you can't moderate it, measure it or monetise it — and off-platform contact is exactly the channel the advance-payment scams run on. If it ships, it ships as a business decision with instrumentation, not as a convenience.

Commercial vehicle

  1. 28

    Carpet area is the primary figure — this one is legal, not aesthetic

    RERA defines carpet area, and marketing property on super built-up has been called out by state authorities as a misleading and unfair trade practice, with penalties running to a percentage of project cost. Show carpet area as the headline number; built-up secondary and explicitly labelled. Add a RERA ID field for regulated projects.

  2. 29

    Only render amenities that are true

    Today booleans always render, so “No Parking” and “No Garden” sit in the same chip style as real features. Filter to true values; if a seller left them unset, say nothing rather than “No”.

  3. 30

    Commercial vehicles finally get their own fields

    bodyType, payloadCapacity, axleCount, seatingCapacity, commercialVehicleType, hasFitness, hasPermit are all parsed today and none reach the screen. Fitness and permit validity are the first two things a fleet buyer checks — a commercial listing without them is unusable.

  4. 31

    Two-wheeler is not the same as a car

    It shares the vehicle template but should drop transmission and show engine cc, and its chips should lead with year · kms · owner. Cheap to add once the section widget exists.

  5. 32

    Stop comparing raw category strings

    'two_wheeler' | 'private_vehicle' | 'commercial_vehicle' | 'property' are compared as literals in five or more places across the page. One enum with an extension for label, icon, spec-section and edit route removes an entire class of silent bugs.

Search results — server-side

  1. 49

    Sort and filter are client-side over one page of 20

    _applyFilters sorts whatever happens to be loaded, and pagination is switched off whenever a filter is active. So “Price: low to high” shows the cheapest of 20 ads, not the cheapest of 312 — and the user has no way to know. This is the single most damaging bug in the browse flow: it silently lies. Move both to the API and restore paging.

    Today search_page.dart:63–86, :1223
  2. 50

    Every keystroke fires a search

    onChanged dispatches straight through with no debounce and no bloc transformer — typing “Innova” is six network calls. Add a 350 ms debounce and restartable(). While you're there: there's no query autocomplete at all, only Google Places suggestions for locations, and the trending list is a hardcoded const.

  3. 51

    No radius filter, no saved searches

    Location currently re-seeds recommendations but can't constrain results — the card shows “4.2 km away” on an ad you can't restrict by distance. And FilterStateService is an in-memory map cleared on exit, so filters die with the process. A “Within 25 km” chip plus a saved search with new-match alerts is the retention loop this app is missing; roughly 89% of marketplaces still don't make saving easy.

  4. 52

    Sort exists in three places with three different UIs

    Chips on category list, pills in the search sheet, nothing at all on home. One sort control, one option set, one place — beside the filter chips, where the result count lives.

  5. 53

    Design tokens exist but are bypassed

    The detail page uses AppTextstyle zero times — every style is an inline TextStyle with a four-breakpoint responsive call, and there are 146 raw Colors.* references across the five browse files. AppColors already has dark-mode getters, so dark mode is broken mostly by bypass. Extend the type scale to include price / body / caption / label roles, add a spacing scale, and the redesign becomes a find-and-replace rather than a rewrite.

Backend

Open questions

  • Does the API have enough comparables to support a price band, or is that a later data project? Ship the band only when it can be right.
  • Keep EMI at all? If there's no lender partner, removing it is cleaner than qualifying it.
  • Is there a moderation step before publish? That decides whether the owner status pill needs “Under review”.
  • Masked call numbers need a telephony provider — worth it, but it's a procurement decision, not a sprint.
  • Should the detail route become /ad/:id? It fixes deep links, share targets and the login round-trip in one change.
  • Response-time badges and offer presets have no published conversion evidence. Ship both, but instrument first-contact rate so the next pass has real numbers.
