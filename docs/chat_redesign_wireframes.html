AdoDad Chat Redesign

Each screen puts what the app does today next to the proposed design. The "Today" phones are rebuilt from chat_rooms_page.dart and chat_page.dart on dev_redesign. The "Proposed" phones fix the findings in chat_system_audit_phase1.md. Colours, type and dark tokens come from app_colors.dart, so the mockups use the app's own palette.

Brand #4F48ECIn-app type PoppinsScreens 14Updated 15 Sep 2026

Assumptions (Phase 2 not signed off)

  • Baseline: dev_redesign + backend develop.
  • Writes over REST with a clientMessageId. Delivery over socket. This is what makes the sending → sent → failed/retry states possible.
  • Phone number stays behind the Call button, as chat_backend_api_spec.md asks. Email is removed from payloads.
  • Typing and online status are drawn but tagged optional. They depend on Q4.
